How SkillLoop Works

A simple 3-step loop designed for busy college students

1
📝

1. Post What You Know

Offer a skill you are confident in — whether it's Python, digital art, calculus, or public speaking. Set your availability and credits.

2
🔍

2. Find What You Need

Browse listings from peers across all engineering and arts departments. Send a quick exchange proposal explaining what you'd like to learn.

3
🎉

3. Exchange & Learn

Once accepted, coordinate a study session in the library or online. Complete the exchange, earn peer karma, and build your campus network.
